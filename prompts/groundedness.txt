by the retrieved evidence. 1.0 = all supported; 0.8 = at least 80% supported with no

Check internal consistency of the evidence: a summary's causal claim is not
supported when its detailed activity records show the opposite failure direction.
Detailed activity observations take precedence over conflicting narrative summary
prose. An answer that correctly explains those observations and rejects the
summary's unsupported conclusion is grounded; do not deduct for disagreeing with
that summary. This precedence applies only where detailed evidence resolves the
conflict, not where the source data itself remains ambiguous.
For example, 'the parent failed because its child failed' supports child-to-parent
propagation; it does NOT support 'the parent's failure spread to its child'.

below 0.8 even if the same contradiction was repeated in a retrieved summary.
An unsupported central claim must score below 0.8, regardless of trivial supported
claims. Without evidence, an honest absence report with no invented facts scores
1.0; substantive unsupported assertions score 0.0.
